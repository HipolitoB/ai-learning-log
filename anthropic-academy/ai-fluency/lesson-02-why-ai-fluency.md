# Lesson 2: Why Do We Need AI Fluency?

**Course:** AI Fluency: Framework and foundations (Anthropic Academy)  
**Date:** 2026-11-24

## Key Takeaways
- Having access to powerful AI models does not automatically translate into effective or safe collaboration.
- AI fluency combines practical skills, domain knowledge, and values centered on **effectiveness, efficiency, ethics, and safety**.
- AI acts not only as a tool, but also as a medium, thinking partner, and co-creator.

## The 3 Modes of Human-AI Collaboration
1. **Automation:** AI executes a specific task based on clear instructions (e.g., summarizing a document, drafting an email). Best when the target outcome is well-defined.
2. **Augmentation:** Iterative collaboration where human and AI think and problem-solve together (e.g., designing software architecture, refining research). Best for open-ended or complex problems.
3. **Agency:** AI operates independently on your behalf using defined knowledge, rules, and behavior patterns—shifting the human role from *scriptwriter* to *director* (e.g., triaging incoming emails or running autonomous workflows).
