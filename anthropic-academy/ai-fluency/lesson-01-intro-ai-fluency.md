# Lesson 1: Introduction to AI Fluency

**Course:** AI Fluency: Framework and foundations (Anthropic Academy)  
**Date:** 2026-11-24

## Key Takeaways
- **AI Fluency:** The ability to collaborate with AI systems in ways that are **effective, efficient, ethical, and safe**.
- **Mindset Shift:** Move from temporary "prompt tricks" to a lasting human-AI collaboration framework that holds up as models evolve.
- **The 4D Framework:**
  1. **Delegation:** Deciding what work to hand to AI vs. keep under human judgment.
  2. **Description:** Communicating goals, context, format, and constraints clearly.
  3. **Discernment:** Critically evaluating AI outputs for accuracy, bias, and usefulness.
  4. **Diligence:** Taking ethical, legal, privacy, and accuracy responsibility for AI-assisted work.

## Initial Reflection
- **Challenges noticed:** Maintaining consistency on complex structured outputs and catching silent hallucinations in technical/trade data.
- **Goal for the course:** Systematize a reliable workflow for integrating AI into data analysis, Python/SQL tooling, and documentation.
