# Lesson 3: The 4D Framework

**Course:** AI Fluency: Framework and foundations (Anthropic Academy)  
**Date:** 2026-11-24

## The 4 Core Competencies (4Ds)
1. **Delegation (The Big Picture):** Strategically dividing work between human and AI based on the goal and what AI does well vs. poorly.
2. **Description (Clear Communication):** Establishing rich context across four dimensions:
   - Desired outcome and output format
   - How the AI should approach the task
   - Relevant context and data needed
   - Tone and interaction style
3. **Discernment (Critical Evaluation):** Using domain expertise to judge accuracy, reasoning, and usefulness. Most real-world work happens in tight **Description ↔ Discernment** iteration loops.
4. **Diligence (Responsible Collaboration):** Ensuring fairness/bias control, fact verification, sensitive-data protection, transparency, and personal accountability for the final work.

## Applied Scenario — Trade & Data Analysis
- **Delegation:** Let AI scaffold data-cleaning or exploratory scripts; keep statistical validation and final commercial conclusions human-led.
- **Description:** Provide exact dataset schema (columns, types, delimiters), analytical question, constraints, and expected output format.
- **Discernment:** Test generated formulas/scripts on real synthetic data to catch silent bugs, null-handling errors, or misinterpretations.
- **Diligence:** Anonymize all confidential supplier/client data before prompting and audit every figure before sharing.
